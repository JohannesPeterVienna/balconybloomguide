Corrections

If a value in a guide or a tool result looks wrong, tell us which page and which number, and if possible your balcony's details. Confirmed errors are corrected and the review date at the bottom of the page changes.

What we cannot do

We cannot assess a specific balcony's load capacity, give legal advice on a lease, or diagnose plant diseases from photos. We do not accept sponsored posts, paid links or product samples.
