How to use the profiles

Start with the light. Each profile gives the direct sun hours the plant needs; the Sun & Shade Simulator tells you which spot on your balcony – railing, middle or back wall – reaches them in which month. Sun lovers such as lavender, rosemary, petunias and tomatoes almost always belong at the railing; fuchsias and bigleaf hydrangeas prefer the shadier back or an east- or north-facing balcony.

Then check the weight. The profiles state the wet weight of typical pot sizes as calculated by the Pot Weight Planner for standard potting mix, because a single large tomato or hydrangea pot can weigh more than a person can lift. Wind and watering follow: the Wind Exposure Check sorts your balcony into one of four classes that the profiles refer to, and the Watering & Holiday Planner estimates how often each pot needs water.

Winter advice is given by USDA hardiness zone. A plant in a pot is more exposed than the same plant in the ground, so the profiles follow the extension advice to plan for about two zones colder. Every profile lists its sources – university extension services and the Missouri Botanical Garden – at the end.
