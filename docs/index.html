The balcony almanac

How much sun does your railing really get?

Plant labels say “full sun” or “part shade”. A balcony has both, a few feet apart: the slab of the balcony above, the side walls and the building across the street decide which spot gets six hours and which gets two. Balcony Bloom Guide calculates it for your balcony – then helps with weight, wind and water.

Why start from the balcony, not the plant

Most gardening advice is written for open ground. A balcony is a box hanging on a wall: the floor above cuts off the high summer sun, the side walls cut off the early and late sun, and the wind at the eighth floor is not the wind at street level. Pots hold a few gallons of mix that dry out in a day, and every gallon of wet mix weighs about six pounds.

So every guide on this site states which spot on the balcony it means, which numbers it relies on and where they come from. The tools work without an account; your balcony profile is stored only in your browser. Read how the calculations work and where their limits are.
