Privacy policy

This policy explains which data is processed when you use balconybloomguide.com, for what purpose and on which legal basis, and what rights you have.

Controller

Hosting

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When a page is loaded, your browser transmits technical data – IP address, requested address, date and time, browser type, referring page – to GitHub's servers, which use it to deliver the page and to protect the service and may keep it in log files for a limited period. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your balcony data in the tools

Inputs in the Sun & Shade Simulator, Pot Weight Planner, Wind Exposure Check and Watering Planner – location, balcony size, pot list – are processed only in your browser. The tools keep your inputs automatically in your browser's local storage on your device, so they are still there on your next visit; “Save as my balcony” additionally stores the calculated sun hours and wind class so that guide pages and the watering planner can use them. They are never sent to us or to third parties. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to see which guides and tools are used, so we can improve them. Before any Google script runs, Google Consent Mode is set to “denied” for advertising and analytics storage, and Google Analytics is switched off. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics is loaded only after you consent in the consent message to storing and accessing information on your device (TCF purpose 1), to Google as a vendor and to analytics. Elsewhere it is loaded only if the consent message reports analytics as granted or not requiring consent and has confirmed that you have not opted out under a U.S. state privacy law; missing or unclear signals keep it switched off. If you withdraw consent via “Privacy settings”, Consent Mode returns to “denied” and Google Analytics is switched off immediately. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
